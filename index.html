How it works

  1. i.

    Write

    Use a mouse, finger or stylus. Arithmetic, algebra, fractions, integrals — write it as you normally would.

  2. ii.

    Solve

    Your sheet is read by Google Gemini, which transcribes the expression and works through it.

  3. iii.

    Follow along

    Every step is typeset properly, so you can see how the answer was reached — not just what it is.
